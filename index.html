Acne & acne scars+

Finding the trigger first (hormones, products, habits), then a plan that clears active acne before any scar work begins.

Pigmentation & uneven tone+

Melasma, tanning and dark patches, treated with the right mix of topicals, peels and sun habits, with no shortcuts that bounce back.

Long-term conditions managed calmly, with clear explanations of flares, triggers and what to expect from treatment.
